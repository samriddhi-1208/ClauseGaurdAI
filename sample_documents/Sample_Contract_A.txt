ENTERPRISE SERVICES AGREEMENT (SAMPLE CONTRACT A)

1. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY POLICY
1.1 Customer Data Retention: All customer data, personal information, and transactional logs processed under this Agreement must be securely retained and archived for a minimum period of 5 (five) years following contract termination or expiration.
1.2 Backup Schedule: Automated daily backups shall be performed and preserved in accordance with the 5-year data retention schedule.

2. PAYMENT & INVOICING TERMS
2.1 Payment Deadline: Net 30 Days. All undisputed invoices issued by Service Provider must be paid in full by Customer within 30 (thirty) calendar days of invoice date.
2.2 Late Interest: Unpaid invoices after 30 days shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
3.1 Non-Disclosure: Each party agrees to hold all proprietary technical and financial information in strict confidence for a period of 3 years.
